What an assistant does

It handles the repetitive messages so you can get on with running the business.

Takes orders the way you do

It asks the same questions you would, checks the details back with the customer, and only records an order once they say yes.

Answers from your own information

Prices, delivery days, minimums and payment options come from your business, not from guesswork. If it does not know, it says so and passes the question to you.

Recognizes regular customers

Returning customers are greeted by name and asked to confirm their address and payment method, never assumed.

Handles changes

Customers can cancel, reschedule or change a pending order by message, and the list updates.

Gives your driver a clean list

Each day's orders are collected into one list, on a phone screen or as a printable page.

How it works

A small, careful build for each business.

  1. We learn how your business runs

    Products, prices, delivery days, how you take payment, and how you like to talk to customers.

  2. We build and test it

    We run real conversations, including awkward ones, and check the records, not just the replies.

  3. You switch it on and stay in charge

    You can see every order, step in at any time, and change how it behaves.

Privacy

When a customer messages a business that uses a Brisavelo assistant, we handle their phone number, name, delivery directions and order details on that business's behalf, only to take and fulfil their order. We do not sell this information.

Messages travel through WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta, and an AI service is used to write replies. This website does not use cookies or analytics. To ask about or remove information held about you, write to us using the contact details above.
